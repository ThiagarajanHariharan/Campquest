## 2024-11-20 - Icon-only Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons using emojis or text symbols (like ✕, 🚪, ☰, ⚙️) are frequently used across the React components but lack inherent accessible names, making them difficult to navigate for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only buttons include descriptive `aria-label` attributes to provide clear context for assistive technologies.
